Privacy policy

Book Swap, last updated October 2026 (added approximate location)

Book Swap is a free, volunteer-run app that helps neighbours swap books. This page explains what information the app collects, why, and how you can delete it.

What we collect

  • A random user ID. Created automatically when you open the app, so it knows which listings are yours. It isn't linked to your phone number, email or real name.
  • Your nickname. Shown to other users instead of your real name.
  • Your listings: book title, author, genre, condition, ISBN, cover picture, area or building, and pickup spot.
  • Swap activity: which books you requested, offered, lent or received, and return dates.
  • Chat messages between the two people in a swap.
  • Reports you send about listings, including the reason and any note.
  • Approximate location of a listing (optional). If you tick "Show how far away this book is" when listing, we save your location at that moment rounded to about 1 km. Other people only see an approximate distance, like "about 2 km away", never a map or your exact spot.

We don't collect your real name, phone number, email address, contacts or precise location. The app shows no ads and has no analytics or tracking.

Camera

The camera is used only when you tap "Scan barcode", to read the ISBN barcode on a book. The video is processed on your phone and is never recorded, stored or uploaded.

Location

When you tap "Near me", your phone's location is used only on your device to sort books by distance. It is never uploaded or stored by us. Your browser asks your permission first, and you can turn location off in your browser or phone settings at any time.

Who can see your information

  • Your nickname, listings, area and pickup spot are visible to everyone using the app.
  • Chat messages are visible only to the two people in that swap.
  • Reports are visible only to the app's admins.

Where it's stored and who processes it

  • Google Firebase stores the app's data and creates the random user ID.
  • Open Library (Internet Archive) and Google Books receive the ISBN number when you scan or type one, to look up the book's details. They don't receive any information about you.
  • Google Fonts and jsDelivr deliver the app's fonts and barcode scanner, as with any website.

We never sell or share your information with anyone else.

Deleting your data

In the app, tap your nickname at the top of the screen, then tap Delete my data. This removes all your listings, cancels your pending swap requests, removes your nickname from past swaps, and deletes your user ID. Chat messages you sent may stay visible to the other person in that chat.

If you can't open the app any more, contact us at the address below and we'll delete your data within 30 days.

Children

Book Swap is meant for people aged 13 and over. Users under 18 should use it with a parent's or guardian's permission.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
